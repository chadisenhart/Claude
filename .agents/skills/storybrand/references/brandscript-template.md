## The One-Liner

**Formula:**
> We help [CHARACTER] who struggle with [PROBLEM] by [YOUR SOLUTION] so they can [SUCCESS OUTCOME].

**Variations to draft:**
- Option A (problem-led): "Most [audience] struggle with [problem]. We [solution] so you can [outcome]."
- Option B (outcome-led): "Imagine [success vision]. That's what we help [audience] achieve."
- Option C (transformation): "We help [audience] go from [before state] to [after state] — without [pain point]."

## Quick Messaging Audit Checklist

Use this to audit existing website/marketing copy:

- [ ] Does the headline say what the customer gets — not what the company does?
- [ ] Is the customer positioned as the hero, not the brand?
- [ ] Is there a clear, prominent direct CTA?
- [ ] Is there a transitional CTA for people not ready to buy?
- [ ] Is the 3-step plan visible on the homepage?
- [ ] Are stakes mentioned anywhere?
- [ ] Is the success/transformation clearly painted?
- [ ] Is empathy expressed before authority?
- [ ] Is the guide (brand) introduced after the problem — not before?
- [ ] Is there a one-liner that could be said at a dinner party?
